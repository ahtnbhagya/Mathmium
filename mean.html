THE MEANING IN EVERY DETAIL

A name, a symbol, a foundation.

Three thoughtful ideas shape the identity of Mathmium and the way it invites learners to explore mathematics.

01
NAME

Mathmium

A place to discover the world of mathematics.

Inspired by the idea of a museum — a place to explore, discover, and learn.

Mathmium is a digital destination where learners can explore the fascinating world of mathematics.

A world of mathematics, ready to be explored.

02
LOGO

Sigma

Every little bit of knowledge adds up.

The Mathmium logo is inspired by the idea of summation: small pieces coming together to create something meaningful.

It reflects the idea that every concept learned, every question answered, and every problem solved adds to a learner’s knowledge, one step at a time.

03
THE TRIANGLES

A Symbol of Strength

Strong foundations. Greater possibilities.

Triangles are among the strongest and most stable shapes used in structural design. Their unique geometry helps structures maintain their shape and resist deformation.

In the same way, a strong foundation in mathematics helps learners develop knowledge, confidence, and problem-solving skills.

Strong foundations, endless possibilities.

MY VISION

Vision
